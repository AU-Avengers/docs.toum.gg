## Town of Us: Mira has updated to [v1.7.0](https://github.com/AU-Avengers/TOU-Mira/releases/tag/1.7.0)!

This update is packed with **big changes and new additions**, including:

- Draft Mode is now part of the base mod!
- A new Impostor and a new Crewmate role!
- 2 new modifiers!
- A new pet!

Also included are **a number of changes to existing roles and modifiers**, as well as bug fixes and improvements. See the full changelog below.

:::danger IMPORTANT

<big>**This update runs on v17.4 of Among Us and <u>requires a full reinstall</u>.**</big>

Do not just replace files as this will cause issues.
:::

<!--truncate-->

---

### New Roles!

- The **Barkeeper** is a _Crewmate Support_ role that can drink with others to roleblock them. They may also spill a drink to speed up or slow down other players.

- The **Bootlegger** is an _Impostor Support_ role that can drink with others to roleblock them. Once a player is roleblocked three times, they will die of poison.

---

### New Modifiers!

- **Drunk** (Universal) — Your controls are permanently inverted. You are also immune to normal roleblocks.
- **Overclocker** (Assailant) — You may decrease your cooldowns at the cost of longer cooldowns once your Overclock is over. After an additional round, your cooldowns will normalize.
